About the condition

Why spiral drawings?

Parkinson’s disease affects dopamine-producing neurons that control movement. Fine motor tasks such as drawing a spiral can reveal early motor changes.

Tremor

Involuntary shaking produces a wavy, uneven line that is hard to see by eye but easy for a CNN to pick up.

Micrographia

Handwriting and drawings tend to shrink and tighten as fine motor control declines.

Slowness & stiffness

Bradykinesia affects stroke smoothness and spacing between the turns of the spiral.
